How this works
Your data stays here. Accounts are saved in this browser on this device — nothing gets uploaded. Clearing browser data wipes the vault, so use Export for backups and Import to restore or merge them.
Rank checks are free. Refresh pulls the current rank straight from op.gg — no account, no key. If the auto-fetch is ever blocked, use Rank in a card's ⋯ menu to enter it by hand — history, the chart and the stats all work the same either way.
Tips: the bar across a card's bottom edge opens Details — flex rank, past seasons and the champions played, each folded until you click it · the star pins favorites on top · search matches names, tags, even ranks (try "gold"), and every word you add narrows it further · statuses mark banned/resting smurfs, banned ones are skipped by checks · tick a card's checkbox to bulk-edit status/tags or delete several at once · press / to search, Esc closes panels.
Quick find: Ctrl+K (or ⌘K) anywhere — type a few letters of a name or a rank to jump straight to an account, or a word like "layout", "export" or "settings" to run it. Arrows move, Enter opens.
Search operators: beyond plain words the search box takes tier:diamond, region:euw, >plat and <gold for "at least" and "below", and is: stale · never · flagged · banned · fav · goal. They combine, so >diamond is:stale is one query.
Three layouts: cards for browsing, the list for working through the vault a row at a time, and the wall for seeing all sixty at once — click any tile to land back on that account's card.
Goals: set a target rank in a card's Rank panel and the card grows a rail showing how far there is left, plus a rough estimate once there's a fortnight of checks to work from.
Extra privacy: set a master password in Settings to encrypt everything at rest, plus an auto-lock timer — without the password, don't lose it, there's no recovery.
Using this app: free for your own personal use, and feel free to share the link. Don't resell it, rebrand it, publish a modified version, or use it for anything malicious or against Riot's terms.
More: ⋯ menu has bulk add (paste a list of Name#TAG) and CSV import · archive an account to hide it without deleting (toggle Archived in the toolbar) · shift-click a card's checkbox to select a range · n for a new account · click a card's note to rewrite it on the spot · pick your own primary and secondary colors in Settings.
