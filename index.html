How this works
Your data stays here. Accounts are saved in this browser on this device — nothing gets uploaded. Clearing browser data wipes the vault, so use Export for backups and Import to restore or merge them.
Rank checks are free. Refresh pulls the current rank straight from op.gg — no account, no key. If the auto-fetch is ever blocked, use Paste (copy the profile page in) or Rank to enter it by hand — history, sparklines and stats work the same either way.
Tips: the star pins favorites on top · search matches names, tags, even ranks (try "gold") · statuses mark banned/resting smurfs, banned ones are skipped by checks · tick a card's checkbox to bulk-edit status/tags or delete several at once · press / to search, Esc closes panels.
Extra privacy: set a master password in Settings to encrypt everything at rest, plus an auto-lock timer — without the password, don't lose it, there's no recovery.
